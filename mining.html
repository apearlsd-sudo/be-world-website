Mining

Responsible mining operations in Tarkwa — plus heavy mining equipment and truck rentals trusted by mining companies, with worker safety and land reclamation built into every program.

Service Overview

Responsible Mining in Tarkwa

We undertake responsible mining activities in Tarkwa — the heart of Ghana's gold country. Every operation starts with a survey and a written plan covering equipment, staffing, safety controls and production targets, and every program is run to international safety and environmental standards, with transparent reporting from day one.

Responsibility is not a slogan here. We prioritize the safety of our workers on every shift, manage our environmental footprint as a daily discipline, and return mined land to a safe, useful state through mine pit and safe land reclamation.

  • Responsible mining operations in Tarkwa
  • Worker safety prioritized on every shift, with documented HSE systems
  • Environmental safety and progressive land reclamation as standard

Equipment Rental

Heavy Equipment & Trucks, Rented to the Industry

We rent heavy mining equipment and trucks to mining companies — including major operators such as Newmont Ghana. Excavators, dump trucks, loaders and support machinery arrive on site serviced, operated by trained crews, and backed by our maintenance team.

For a mine, uptime is everything. Our rental fleet is managed on a strict preventive-maintenance schedule with standby arrangements on critical machines, so your production plan does not wait for a breakdown.

  • Excavators, haul trucks and loaders available on contract
  • Trusted by mining companies such as Newmont Ghana
  • Preventive maintenance and trained operators included

Scope of Service

What Our Mining Service Includes

Responsible Mining Operations

Open-pit mining in Tarkwa run to a written operating plan — survey first, production targets agreed, work supervised.

Heavy Equipment & Truck Rental

Mining equipment and trucks rented to mining companies — serviced, staffed and supported by our own maintenance team.

Excavation & Haulage

Free-dig and excavator operations with load-and-carry cycles matched to pit geometry and haul profile.

Workers Safety

We prioritize our people: PPE discipline, risk assessments, daily inspections and crews empowered to stop unsafe work.

Environmental Safety

Dust suppression, water management and waste controls that keep the surrounding land and communities protected.

Mine Pit & Safe Land Reclamation

Progressive reshaping, soil restoration and revegetation — mined land returned to a safe, stable and useful state.

Reclamation

When the Mining Ends, the Land Lives On

A mine's last task is to give the land back. Our mine pit and safe land reclamation work reshapes depleted pits, restores soils and revegetates disturbed ground — closing out sites that are stable, safe for communities and ready for a productive afterlife, from farmland to forest.

  • Pit reshaping and slope stabilization to agreed closure plans
  • Soil restoration and revegetation with suitable species
  • Handover-ready land documented for regulators and communities
